# Aficion: product one-pager

**What it is:** A constellation atlas of hobbies: highlight yours, trace the paths between them, and compare maps with friends

**Who it is for:** The person with four shelves in one room. A half-built
Gundam, a soldering iron, a handheld radio and a camera. They have never seen
the four laid out as one object, so when someone asks what they do they list
four unconnected nouns and stop. Aficion is for them, and for the friend they
send the link to.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A laptop propped on a workbench at eleven at night, room light
low, between two sessions on the same half-finished thing. Read at arm's length,
driven with one hand, on a screen that also has a soldering iron next to it.
That is why the canvas is near-black, why the map is dense rather than airy, and
why every control sits within a thumb of where the eye already is.

**What done looks like:** The visitor has marked the things they actually do,
seen a gold path appear between two of them they thought were unrelated, and
either copied the link or added one node they had not considered adjacent.

---

## The four promises

1. **One connected fabric.** Every cluster reaches the hub, and the crafts
   underneath the hobbies are on the same map rather than in a separate legend.
2. **Your own path, lit.** Marking two adjacent things draws the line between
   them. Marking two distant things says how many steps apart they are, and
   through what.
3. **A reason, not a ranking.** A suggestion arrives with the sentence that
   justifies it, built from shared crafts, shared tags or shared gear, and the
   map rings the node that sentence names, in its own colour. There is no score
   anywhere in the product.
4. **A map you can hand to someone.** The whole profile is a URL fragment, which
   browsers never send to a server. Compare reads a friend's link in the tab and
   never writes it anywhere.

## What it deliberately is not

- **Not a learning plan.** `skill-map-site` is the roadmap product, with XP,
  levels and streaks. Aficion's levels describe how deep a hobby goes, never
  points earned, and the two sites share no vocabulary.
- **Not a compatibility score.** Compare returns four sets and a tally counted
  the same way the map's own panel counts, one row per cluster plus the crafts
  and the centre. No percentage, no total, no ranking. Two people's hobbies are
  not a league table.
- **Not an account.** No backend, no sign-in, no sync. Local storage holds your
  map; a link carries it.
