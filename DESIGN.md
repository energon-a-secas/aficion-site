# Aficion interface

## Product and scene

A person follows an interest on a laptop after work, or on a phone between projects. The dark atlas stays comfortable in a dim room. Reading and choosing happen on quiet, solid surfaces; colour belongs to their saved hobbies and to meaningful connections.

## Structure

- Explore is the first visit: a search, a small set of directions, and readable category lists.
- The homepage demonstrates breadth and depth with three selectable, authored examples. Each has a four-stop path across interests, a real side branch, a link into a subtopic and an entrance to the same guided route in the Atlas. On phones, search precedes the example in both visual and reading order; the preview steps aside while searching.
- Atlas is the spatial view. Its zoom, focus and advanced controls remain available without becoming prerequisites for browsing.
- Atlas selection emphasises connections. Every authored edge is represented by a small arrow at its true bearing, or a counted group when paths crowd together. Visual circles are 25px; their hit areas are 42px. Never silently discard destinations to fit four directions. Teal identifies local branches, cyan shared crafts, violet other regions, and gold the exploration trail and entrance to depth. A trail supports backtracking and fitting the whole route. Keep saving quiet and motion finite, with an immediate reduced-motion alternative.
- Region focus shows the entire local family. Region names, an overview link, and the section picker preserve orientation. Inner trees are interactive diagrams with matching numbered lists; selecting a subtopic explores it, and saving requires its separate bookmark.
- Trails are optional, behind a quiet map entry. Suggested routes unfold into numbered stops with an explanation of each connection. A guide appears only after opening a route: future connections are dashed gold, visited ones solid gold, and unrelated connections recede. Taking a side branch leaves the guide and keeps the actual exploration trail. Named routes persist separately from saved hobbies; opening or saving a route never allocates its stops. The compact phone dock replaces the ordinary selected-node card while guiding.
- Your hobbies holds only choices the visitor made. Examples are explicitly opened, never automatically saved.
- Breadcrumbs connect direction, category, hobby and technique. Browser Back retraces the exploration.
- Connections have four lenses: related pursuits, shared skills, shared equipment and shared interests. Each result explains its relationship. Shared interests are an inference from tags, not an authored equipment connection.

## Visual rules

Use the shared Neorgon font, tokens, header, footer and theme support. The local accent is warm gold. New surfaces use restrained, tinted neutrals in OKLCH and inherit light themes. Body copy stays below 72 characters per line. Titles use the same sans family as controls, at 40, 28, 20 and 16px. Body copy is 15 or 16px, secondary text at least 13px.

Navigation and hobby results are rows, with generous clickable areas and full borders only where they clarify a boundary. Avoid nested cards, decorative glass, automatic tours and page-load animation. Reserve small diagrams for real relationships. Use semantic links for navigation, buttons for actions, visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.

## Progressive detail

Show the description, save action and first experiment before commitment settings. Subtopics are independent interests, not a ranked curriculum. Show a few connections and allow expansion. Preserve the selected lens and scroll position during saving. On phones the browsing experience uses normal document-like scrolling inside the workspace; the atlas has its own explicit entry point.
